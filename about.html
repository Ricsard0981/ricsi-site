About me

Hey, I'm Ricsi.

I'm an independent creator from Hungary who loves building things across different creative fields. From videos and music to software, games and Minecraft projects, I like turning ideas into real things people can watch, hear or play.

My YouTube journey goes back further than my current channel. After my original channel was hacked and banned in 2022, I started over from scratch. That restart became a new chapter, and I've kept experimenting with content, editing and storytelling ever since.

I don't want to limit myself to one medium. I want to keep learning, build ambitious projects and grow a creative identity that connects everything I make. This site is my archive of that.

The channel

My channel is Hungarian Minecraft content where I experiment with storytelling and editing: gameplay with real pacing, sound design and motion graphics.

I'm working toward long-form videos. There's no fixed upload schedule yet, because I'd rather finish a video properly than upload something that feels unfinished.

What I work with

Editing is how I express myself: fast storytelling, carefully timed cuts, motion graphics and sound design that make a video feel alive.

On the code side I started with HTML and CSS at school, moved on to Python, and I'm now in my second year of C#. I also build games in Godot, make Minecraft mods and plugins, and produce hyperpop as rix.off.
