My story

Hey, I'm Ricsi.

I'm an independent creator from Hungary who loves building things across different creative fields. From videos and music to software, games and Minecraft projects, I enjoy turning ideas into real experiences.

My YouTube journey goes back further than my current channel. After my original channel was hacked and banned in 2022, I started over from scratch. That restart became the beginning of a new chapter under Clivix.

I don't want to limit myself to one medium. I want to keep learning, build ambitious projects and develop a creative identity that connects everything I make. This website is my archive of that journey.

YT
Clivix — YouTube

Clivix

Clivix is my Hungarian Minecraft channel, where I experiment with content, storytelling and editing. I want videos that feel engaging, intentional and memorable, combining gameplay with pacing, sound design, motion graphics and a distinctive visual style.

I'm working toward a new creative direction focused on long-form videos. There's no fixed upload schedule yet, because I'm learning to balance the vision in my head with a sustainable creative process, so I can keep making work I'm genuinely proud of.
